Redesign: B2B Onboarding Flow with 60% Drop-off
The Situation
You're joining a B2B SaaS company as a senior product designer. The company's onboarding flow has a critical drop-off problem.
Company Context

Series A project management tool for agencies ($8M raised)
Target: Creative agencies (10-100 employees)
Current users: 2,000 teams
Team: 1 PM, 2 engineers, 1 designer (you)

Current Onboarding Problem

Sign-up to "first value" conversion: 40% (industry avg: 65%)
60% of users drop off before completing setup
Average time to complete onboarding: 12 minutes
Support tickets about onboarding: 35% of all tickets

Current Flow (5 steps)

Account creation (email, password, company name)
Team setup (invite 3+ teammates)
Integration setup (connect Slack, Google, Figma)
First project creation (template or blank)
Tutorial walkthrough (5-minute interactive guide)

User Research Highlights

"I just wanted to try it myself first before inviting my team"
"The integrations step felt overwhelming — too many options"
"I didn't understand why I needed to create a project to see the tool"
"The tutorial was actually helpful but I almost didn't get there"

Business Constraints

Team invites are critical for virality (30% of new signups come from invites)
Integrations drive retention (users with 2+ integrations have 3x better retention)
Engineering capacity: 1 sprint (2 weeks) for V1 improvements

Your Task
Redesign the onboarding experience to meaningfully improve sign-up to first-value conversion. The current rate is 40%; the goal is a defensible improvement, not a hero number.
What to Submit
Your redesign should include:

Problem Analysis

What's actually causing the 60% drop-off? Which steps need the most attention?
What assumptions are baked into the current flow?
What would you want to learn before committing to a direction, and what are you willing to commit to without more research?


Redesigned Flow & Design Decisions

New step-by-step flow (wireframes or detailed descriptions — hand-drawn or low-fi is fine)
How do you handle the team invite step and preserve integration adoption without blocking activation?
How do you get users to value faster? How do you reintroduce "skipped" steps later?
What trade-offs are you making, and what are you deliberately not solving in V1?


V1 Scope & Success Metrics

What's the minimum viable improvement for a 2-week sprint? What would you cut?
How would you measure if this works? What guardrail metrics would tell you the redesign failed even if conversion went up?
